PRODUCT OVERVIEW & TECHNICAL EXPLAINER
================================================================================

1. WHAT IS ER-SYNC AND WHAT DOES IT DO?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In many major cities (like Lagos, Nigeria), emergency healthcare suffers from 
a deadly problem known as "Blind Patient Routing":
- When an accident or medical emergency happens, ambulances and families rush to 
  the nearest hospital without knowing if that hospital has an available bed, 
  an open operating theatre, or a specialist (like a neurosurgeon or cardiologist) 
  on duty.
- If the hospital is full or cannot handle the case, the patient is turned away.
- Patients often spend critical hours being shuttled from one hospital to another, 
  frequently losing their lives in transit.

ER-Sync is an Emergency Medical Operations & Dispatch Network that solves this. 
It connects emergency responders, state emergency command centers, and hospitals 
into one live, synchronized grid.

What ER-Sync does in real time:
1. Instant Incident Intake: Responders or dispatchers type or dictate what happened.
2. Clinical AI Triage: The system reads the messy description, calculates medical 
   severity, and identifies required specialists and surgical equipment.
3. Optimal Hospital Matching: In less than 1 second, it scans all registered 
   regional hospitals, checks live bed counts, on-duty doctors, and Lagos traffic 
   ETAs, and picks the best facility.
4. Guaranteed Bed Reservation: It alerts the receiving hospital and reserves a 
   trauma bed before the ambulance even arrives.
5. Instant Auto-Failover: If Hospital #1 suddenly rejects the referral due to a 
   surge, ER-Sync automatically reroutes the patient to Candidate #2 in under 
   500 milliseconds without human delay.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ER-Sync is built with a fast, modern, and lightweight tech stack:

A. FRONTEND (User Interface):
   - Framework: React 19 (modern functional components & hooks)
   - Language: TypeScript (strict type safety)
   - Build Tool: Vite 6 (instant hot reloading and fast build times)
   - Styling: Modern Light-Mode Design System (Tailwind CSS v4 + Vanilla CSS)

3. HOW THE AI IS GENERATIVE & WHAT IT GENERATES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ER-Sync uses a smart two-layer AI architecture: a Generative AI Layer for clinical 
understanding, followed by a Deterministic Safety Layer for the actual routing decision.

A. Why Generative AI is used:
   When emergency calls arrive, people don't speak in neat database codes. 
   A caller might say:
   "There was a terrible okada crash on Third Mainland Bridge! The rider was thrown 
    into a barrier, his head is bleeding heavily, he is not waking up at all, and 
    his right leg looks bent out of shape!"

   Traditional software cannot understand that paragraph. Generative AI can.

B. What the Generative AI generates:
   Powered by Large Language Models (such as OpenAI's GPT-4o-mini, with an offline 
   clinical NLP fallback):
   
   1. Generates Structured Severity Classification:
      Translates chaotic wording into standard Emergency Severity Index (ESI):
      -> Result: "CRITICAL" (Immediate resuscitation required)

   2. Generates Mandatory Specialist Requirements:
      Analyzes the injury descriptions and dynamically outputs which medical 
      specialties must be at the hospital:
      -> Generated: ["Neurosurgery", "Orthopaedics", "Surgery", "Anaesthesia"]

   3. Generates Required Diagnostic & Surgical Facilities:
      Determines what equipment the patient will need immediately:
      -> Generated: ["CT Scanner", "Operating Theatre", "Emergency Department", "Blood Bank"]

   4. Generates Clinical Rationale & Explanation:
      Synthesizes an easy-to-read medical justification so doctors at the hospital 
      understand why this triage grade was given:
      -> Generated: "High suspicion of traumatic brain injury with active intracranial 
         bleed and compound lower limb fracture. Immediate neurosurgical decompression 
         and cross-matched blood needed."

C. Why the Generative AI does NOT pick the hospital:
   In medical emergencies, an LLM must NEVER hallucinate or guess hospital beds. 
   If an LLM picks a hospital that is actually full, a patient dies.
   
   Therefore, the Generative AI ONLY generates the medical requirement blueprint. 
   Then, our Deterministic Mathematical Engine takes over to match that blueprint 
   against live database telemetry (free beds, on-duty doctors, travel ETA in traffic) 
   with 100% mathematical precision and zero hallucinations.
